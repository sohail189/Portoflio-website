About me

From data to deployed AI systems

Hi, I'm Sohail — an AI Engineer and Data Scientist who enjoys taking messy, real-world data and turning it into something people can actually use. I focus on building and deploying practical machine learning and LLM-based applications, not just prototypes that stay in a notebook.

My project work spans classical ML (classification, XGBoost, feature engineering), NLP and generative AI (RAG pipelines with LangChain, ChromaDB, and Groq), and backend deployment with FastAPI, Docker, and CI/CD. Each project goes from raw data through to a working, deployed application.

I'm currently looking for Data Analyst and Data Engineer opportunities, with particular interest in the Gulf region, where I can keep building end-to-end data and AI systems.
